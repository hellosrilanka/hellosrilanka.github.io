Travel at your pace, with local insight.

Hello Sri Lanka helps visitors discover the island comfortably, whether you're interested in cultural landmarks, tea country, wildlife, beaches or local food. As a friendly, smart, and fluent English-speaking local guide, I can coordinate your perfect Sri Lankan vacation seamlessly.

Hotels & attractions guide

We can help you shortlist accommodation areas and places to visit based on your route, travel dates and budget.

🏨 Where to stay

  • Colombo / Negombo: useful for arrival, departure and city stays.
  • Sigiriya / Dambulla: a convenient base for the Cultural Triangle and nearby heritage sites.
  • Kandy: suitable for temple visits, gardens and cultural experiences.
  • Ella / Nuwara Eliya: mountain scenery, tea country and cooler weather.
  • Galle / Hiriketiya: historic coastal stays, beaches and relaxed cafés.

🗺️ Attractions to consider

  • History: Sigiriya Rock Fortress, Dambulla Cave Temple, Temple of the Tooth and Galle Fort.
  • Nature: Nine Arch Bridge, Little Adam's Peak, tea estates and waterfalls.
  • Wildlife: Yala or Udawalawe National Park with a suitable safari provider.
  • Beach time: Hiriketiya, Unawatuna, Mirissa or Trincomalee, depending on your route and season.
  • Custom plan: tell us your dates, preferred hotel standard and interests to discuss options.
Hotel names, room rates, availability, attraction opening times and entry fees can change. Confirm them for your travel dates before booking. Official Sri Lanka tourism and Central Cultural Fund links are provided on relevant destination cards.
